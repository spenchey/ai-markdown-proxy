# Trade In a Car You Still Owe Money On - Carroll, Iowa

Yes, you can trade in your car even if you still owe money on the loan. We handle the payoff with your lender.

## What we need from you

To get started, we need some information:

- The name of your loan holder.
- The payoff amount on your loan, or permission for us to request it from your lender.
- Your car's current mileage.
- A description of your car's condition.

## Negative equity

If the amount you owe on your car loan is more than the car’s trade-in value, this is called negative equity. The difference can be added to the price of your next vehicle, subject to lender approval.
