## Consider a Used Car

A used car is often a good choice for a new driver. Used cars are generally more affordable. This can lower your overall cost. Remember to factor in insurance and maintenance. These costs add to the total price of owning a car.

## What to Look for in a Used Car

- **Mileage:** Lower mileage usually means less wear and tear.
- **Condition:** Inspect the car carefully. Look for any damage.
- **Vehicle History Report:** Get a vehicle history report. This report can show accidents or other issues.
- **Second Key:** Having two keys is helpful.
- **Tires:** Check the tire tread. New tires can be an added expense.

## Budgeting for a Car

Don't just think about the monthly payment. Consider:

- **Insurance:** Get insurance quotes *before* you shop.
- **Maintenance:** Factor in regular maintenance costs.
- **Fuel:** Estimate your fuel costs.
- **Registration and Taxes:** These are additional expenses.
