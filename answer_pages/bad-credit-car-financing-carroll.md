# Bad Credit Car Financing in Carroll, Iowa

Motor Inn Auto Group works with customers who have bad credit or no credit.

## How approval works here

Our finance team reviews each application individually. Financing is subject to lender approval and current program terms. We submit your information to multiple lenders. They consider your credit history and other factors. We work to find an option that fits your needs.

## What helps your application

Having trade equity can help. A down payment can also improve your chances. Stable income is important. Trade values depend on vehicle, payoff, condition and market.
