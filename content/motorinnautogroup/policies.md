## Machine-readable content rules

- DealerOn's existing VDP price and availability disclosure must appear verbatim below every generated inventory table.
- DealerOn's existing eligibility, expiration, and legal disclosure must appear verbatim for every generated offer.
- Customer forms, consent language, privacy controls, and data-deletion flows remain on the canonical human pages.
- Do not place personal information, credit application data, trade-form submissions, or customer records in Markdown resources.
- Machine-readable files complement the human site; they do not replace signed purchase documents, lender terms, OEM program rules, or dealership confirmation.
