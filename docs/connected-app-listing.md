# Google Connected Apps listings

Use one Connected App per store. These values are for the Google Connected Console and are intentionally short enough to paste directly. For each store, choose **Automotive** as the category, **MCP server** as the connection type, and **No authorization**.

## Motor Inn of Carroll

- **Connected app name:** `@Motor Inn of Carroll`
- **Category:** `Automotive`
- **Description:** `Find Carroll Motor Inn inventory, read location, service, and parts information, and submit one customer lead request.`
- **Capabilities:**
  - `Find Vehicle Inventory`
  - `Read Service Information`
  - `Read Parts Information`
  - `Submit A Customer Lead`
- **Example prompts:**
  - `@Motor Inn of Carroll, how many used Silverado trucks do you have under $30,000?`
  - `@Motor Inn of Carroll, where do I go for oil service?`
  - `Can I get the location for Motor Inn of Carroll?`
- **MCP server URL:** `https://ai.motorinnofcarroll.com/mcp`
- **Terms of service URL:** `https://ai.motorinnofcarroll.com/terms`
- **Policy URL:** `https://ai.motorinnofcarroll.com/privacy`
- **Icon:** `https://ai.motorinnofcarroll.com/assets/icon-512.png`
