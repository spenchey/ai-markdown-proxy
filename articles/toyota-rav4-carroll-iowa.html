What 2026 RAV4 specs matter for everyday use?

The useful specs are the ones tied to your actual driving: drivetrain, cargo needs, ground clearance, and trailer weight.

  • Toyota lists 226 net combined horsepower for the front-wheel-drive hybrid and 236 for the all-wheel-drive hybrid.
  • The LE has 8.1 inches of ground clearance.
  • Toyota lists a 1,750-pound towing capacity for the all-wheel-drive LE.
  • Toyota lists a 3,500-pound towing capacity for the all-wheel-drive XLE Premium.

Those ratings are grade- and drivetrain-specific. Confirm the exact RAV4, installed equipment, and owner information before towing.

Which 2026 RAV4 trims use SofTex?

Toyota lists SofTex-trimmed seats on the 2026 XLE Premium and Woodland, while other grades use fabric or a SofTex/synthetic-suede combination.

SofTex is Toyota’s material name; Toyota’s specification pages say “SofTex-trimmed,” not leather. Check the exact grade and model year because upholstery changes.
