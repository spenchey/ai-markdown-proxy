Is the Grand Highlander Bigger Than the Highlander?

Yes. The 2026 Toyota Grand Highlander is 6.5 inches longer and has a 3.9-inch-longer wheelbase than the 2026 Highlander. It also provides more cargo room and 5.5 inches more third-row legroom.

Which has more room behind the third row?

The 2026 Grand Highlander has 20.6 cubic feet behind its third row, compared with 16.0 cubic feet in the 2026 Highlander.

That 4.6-cubic-foot difference matters when all rows are in use. With the rear rows folded, the Grand Highlander’s advantage grows to 13.2 cubic feet.

Which has more third-row legroom?

The Grand Highlander has 33.5 inches of third-row legroom, which is 5.5 inches more than the Highlander’s 28.0 inches.

The measurement does not decide comfort for every passenger, but it gives frequent third-row users more space to work with. Sit in both rows before deciding.

Is the Highlander easier to fit in a garage?

The Highlander takes up less length because its body is 6.5 inches shorter.

Measure your garage, including the closed door, storage, and walking room. Width and mirror clearance matter too. Do not assume either SUV fits from length alone.

Which is better if you use the third row often?

The Grand Highlander is the stronger fit when the third row and cargo area are both used often.

The Highlander can make more sense when the third row is occasional and the shorter body better fits your parking or garage. This is a size choice, not a claim that one model is better for every family.

Do both the Highlander and Grand Highlander seat the same number of people?

Both 2026 models offer seating for up to eight, with capacity depending on the selected grade and second-row seat layout.

Check the exact vehicle’s seating configuration before you buy. A second-row bench and captain’s chairs do not carry the same number of passengers.
